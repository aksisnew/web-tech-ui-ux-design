## COLOR SCHEMES being used

1. Faded blue
2. Faded red
3. Faded pink
4. Faded purple.

the idea of faded colors is because it gives a unique and and bright look.

## COLORS I AVOID

1. cyan, green, orange, black, grey - because it gives a neon look we are going for a very subtle, bright, cool and vibrant look but without neon.


## Scheme in ui

1. Border Definition: Use crisp 1rem-scaling borders with subtle contrast (border-white/20 or border-black/10) to separate panels cleanly without relying on ambient shadows.
2. Surface Separation: Achieve visual depth purely through color layering (Darker Faded Blue base background -> Lighter Faded Pink/Purple card overlays)
3. Default Task State: Faded Blue container background with crisp white typography for soft contrast.
4. Disabled buttons or areas are distinguished by subtle background of blue or red and sharp border so the card or ui item is distinguishable from the background color.
